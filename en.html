      01 / CAPABILITIES

      How the agent works

      Check sources, prioritize existing captions, and use transcripts when needed. Select supported excerpts, merge coverage of the same event, and rank by usefulness, impact, and recency.

      02 / STANDARDS

      What makes a handoff complete?

      Every input has a traceable outcome. Merged items retain their sources. Key details and conditions stay intact. Up to three verified items get a ⭐.

      03 / LIMITS

      When is human review needed?

      Restricted sources, unclear audio, possible duplicates, and unresolved conflicts are flagged. Unknown fields stay blank. The agent resumes on request.
